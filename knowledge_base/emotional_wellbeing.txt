# Emotional Wellbeing and Mood Regulation
Source: Cognitive Behavioural and Mindfulness-Based Wellbeing Frameworks

Emotional wellbeing is the ability to navigate emotions with awareness, acceptance, and compassion rather than suppression or judgment.

Core Wellbeing Strategies:
1. Emotion Labeling (Name it to Tame it):
Accurately labeling an emotion (e.g., "I am feeling disappointed and exhausted") reduces amygdala reactivity and engages the prefrontal cortex for problem-solving.

2. Behavioral Activation:
When mood is low, there is often a natural urge to withdraw and become inactive, which deepens depressive cycles. Intentionally scheduling small, meaningful activities (a short walk, making tea, listening to upbeat music) helps kickstart positive dopaminergic feedback.

3. Reflective Journaling:
Expressive writing for 10-15 minutes can clarify confusing emotions, externalize ruminative thoughts, and provide mental perspective.

4. Self-Compassion Practice:
Treat yourself with the same warmth, empathy, and patience that you would offer to a close friend facing a similar hardship.
