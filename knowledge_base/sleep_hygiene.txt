# Sleep Hygiene and Restorative Sleep Practices
Source: Evidence-Based Sleep Medicine and Behavioural Wellbeing Standards

Restful sleep is foundational for emotional resilience, memory consolidation, and hormonal balance.

Essential Sleep Hygiene Recommendations:
1. Consistent Sleep Schedule:
Go to bed and wake up at the exact same times every day, even on weekends. Consistent circadian timing strengthens your natural sleep drive.

2. Wind-Down Routine (60 Minutes Before Bed):
Dim bright overhead lights, avoid stressful emails or intense work, and engage in calming activities such as reading physical books or gentle stretching.

3. Limit Screen Light Exposure:
Blue light emitted by smartphones, laptops, and televisions suppresses melatonin secretion. Cease electronic screen use at least 45-60 minutes before bedtime.

4. Optimize Sleep Environment:
Ensure the bedroom is cool (around 18-20°C / 65-68°F), dark, and quiet. Use blackout curtains or earplugs if needed.

5. Stimulant Management:
Avoid caffeine, nicotine, and heavy meals within 6 hours of bedtime. While alcohol can induce drowsiness, it severely disrupts REM sleep architecture.

6. The 20-Minute Rule:
If unable to sleep after 20 minutes in bed, get up, move to a dim room, do a quiet low-stimulation activity, and only return to bed when genuinely sleepy.
