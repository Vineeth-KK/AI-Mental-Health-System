# Anxiety Management and Calming Protocols
Source: Clinical Psychological Guidelines for Anxiety Coping Strategies

Anxiety involves anticipatory worry, physical tension, and overestimation of future threats.

Effective Grounding and Calming Protocols:
1. The 5-4-3-2-1 Sensory Grounding Technique:
When feeling overwhelmed, anchor your awareness in the present moment by naming:
- 5 things you can see around you
- 4 things you can physically touch (texture of clothing, cold desk)
- 3 things you can hear (clock ticking, distant breeze)
- 2 things you can smell
- 1 thing you can taste or a positive affirmation

2. 4-7-8 Breathing Technique:
Inhale quietly through your nose for 4 counts, hold your breath gently for 7 counts, and exhale completely through your mouth making a whoosh sound for 8 counts. Repeat for 4 full breath cycles.

3. Thought Challenging:
Distinguish between productive worry (actionable problems you can address right now) and unproductive worry (hypothetical "what-ifs"). For hypothetical worries, practice radical acceptance and return your focus to the present task.

4. De-escalating Physiological Panic:
Remind yourself: "These physical sensations (racing heart, shallow breathing) are uncomfortable, but they are not dangerous and they will pass within minutes."
