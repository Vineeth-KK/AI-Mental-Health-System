# Crisis Support and Professional Mental Health Guidance
Source: International Health Authorities and Crisis Helplines Directory

If you or someone you know is in acute psychological distress, experiencing unbearable pain, or having thoughts of self-harm or suicide, immediate human support is available around the clock. You do not have to carry this alone.

Official Emergency Helplines (Confidential, Free, 24/7):

1. United States & Canada:
- Call or Text: 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline)
- Crisis Text Line: Text HOME to 741741
- Website: https://988lifeline.org

2. India:
- Tele-MANAS (Govt of India 24/7 National Tele-Mental Health): 14416 or 1800-891-4416
- Vandrevala Foundation Helpline: +91 9999 666 555
- KIRAN Mental Health Helpline: 1800-599-0019

3. United Kingdom:
- Emergency: 999
- Non-emergency NHS: 111
- Samaritans Helpline: Call 116 123
- Shout Crisis Text Service: Text SHOUT to 85258

4. International Directory:
- Befrienders Worldwide: https://www.befrienders.org
- International Association for Suicide Prevention (IASP): https://www.iasp.info/resources/Crisis_Centres/

When to Seek Immediate Professional Help:
- Thoughts of wanting to die or hurt oneself
- Feeling trapped, hopeless, or having no reason to live
- Withdrawing completely from loved ones and saying goodbyes
- Severe sudden changes in mood or uncontrollable panic
Please reach out to emergency medical services or proceed immediately to the nearest hospital emergency department.
