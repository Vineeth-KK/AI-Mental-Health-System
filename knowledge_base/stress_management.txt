# Stress Management and Relaxation Techniques
Source: Public Health & Evidence-Based Psychological Wellbeing Resources

Stress is the body's natural reaction to challenges or demands. While acute stress can sharpen focus, chronic stress can strain emotional and physical health.

Key Coping Strategies:
1. Box Breathing (Square Breathing):
Inhale for 4 seconds, hold for 4 seconds, exhale for 4 seconds, and hold for 4 seconds. Repeat for 4 cycles. This activates the parasympathetic nervous system and decreases heart rate.

2. Progressive Muscle Relaxation (PMR):
Systematically tense and then release muscle groups from feet to head. Notice the contrast between tension and relaxation to release accumulated physical strain.

3. Cognitive Reframing:
Identify unhelpful thought distortions (such as catastrophizing or black-and-white thinking). Ask: "What is an alternative, balanced perspective on this situation?"

4. Physical Movement:
Moderate aerobic exercise such as brisk walking, swimming, or gentle yoga for 20-30 minutes helps metabolize stress hormones (cortisol and adrenaline).

5. Healthy Boundaries:
Learn to say no to non-essential commitments when feeling overwhelmed. Schedule dedicated rest periods throughout the week.
